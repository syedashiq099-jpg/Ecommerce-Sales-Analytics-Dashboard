# Ecommerce-Sales-Analytics-Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze sales performance, 
profitability, and customer behavior for an e-commerce business. The 
dashboard provides state-wise sales breakdowns, category and sub-category 
performance, payment mode trends, and month-over-month profit analysis to 
support data-driven business decisions.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, graphs, KPI cards, slicers, and interactive visuals

## ✨ Key Features

- 📊 **Interactive Ecommerce Sales Dashboard** – Provides a consolidated view of overall sales performance.
- 💰 **KPI Analysis** – Displays Total Profit, Total Quantity, Total Amount, and Average metrics.
- 🗺️ **State-wise Sales Analysis** – Compares total sales across different states.
- 🛍️ **Category Performance** – Analyzes sales contribution of major product categories.
- 📅 **Monthly Profit Analysis** – Visualizes profit performance across different months.
- 👥 **Top Customer Analysis** – Displays the top 6 customers based on sales amount.
- 💳 **Payment Mode Analysis** – Shows the distribution of payments through COD, UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, and EMI.
- 📦 **Sub-Category Analysis** – Compares sales performance across product sub-categories.
- 🔎 **Interactive Filters** – Includes Quarter and Month filters for dynamic analysis.
- 📈 **Business Performance Insights** – Helps identify sales trends, profitable periods, customer performance, and product/category contributions.
